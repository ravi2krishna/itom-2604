    -> Workflow: Work + Flow 
        (How Work Moves From One Step To Another Until it's finished)

    -> Real Life Use Case (Ordering Food Online)

        -> You Place Order 

        -> Restaurant Receives Order 

        -> Food Is Prepared 

        -> Delivery Partner Picks Up  

        -> Food Delivered 

        -> Review 

    
    -> In Terms Of Business / In Company 

        -> A Workflow is the path a task follows from 

            Request -> Approval -> Action -> Completion 

        
        -> IT Support Workflow 

            One Of The Employees Says: My Laptop Is Not Working 

            Step - 1 

                -> Employee Raises A Ticket 

                    My Laptop is not starting - submitted via IT Portal/Help desk tool

                    Ticket Created In System (*)

            Step - 2
                
                -> Manager Approval

                    Manager Reviews Request and Approves 

                    Auto / Mandatory / Optional (*)
            
            Step - 3

                -> IT Team Receives Request  

                    Ticket is assigned to IT Help desk Queue 

                    SLA Timer Starts (*)

            Step - 4

                -> Technician Checks Issue 

                    Technician Visits Employee or Connects Remotely (Hardware / Software)

                    Technician Assigned (*)

            Step - 5
                
                -> Laptop Needs Repair / Replacement 

                    Issue Fixed On Spot / A Replacement Device is Provided from IT Inventory 

                    Resolution Captured (*)

            Step - 6

                -> Ticket Closed 

                Employee Confirms Issue is resolved, ticket is closed 

                Workflow Completed (*)
